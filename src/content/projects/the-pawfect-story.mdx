## Overview

The Pawfect Story sells accessories for dogs and cats: harnesses, leashes, beds, bowls, grooming and toys. The brand is playful, so the store leans on animated illustration while keeping the path to checkout short.

## What was built

- A **decoupled storefront**: WordPress runs on its own dashboard subdomain as the content and catalogue backend, and customers only ever see the custom frontend.
- **21 browsable categories**, bestseller and new-arrival rails, and products with multiple variants shown at card level.
- **Customer accounts, a wishlist** and a mobile bottom navigation.
- A **blog** targeting buying-guide searches ("best dog toys in India", teething toys, puppy treats).

## Stack

A custom storefront on top of headless WordPress, with Google Tag Manager for analytics.
