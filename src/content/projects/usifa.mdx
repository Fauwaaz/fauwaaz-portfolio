## Overview

USIFA mobilises corporations and philanthropists to fund programmes that strengthen US–India ties. Its audience is donors and corporate partners, so credibility and transparency matter as much as the stories.

## What was built

- An **initiatives system**: each programme has its own page, surfaced on the homepage and in an initiatives index.
- **Monthly Spotlight** and **updates** sections for ongoing storytelling.
- A **reports page** for financial and annual reports, a downloadable brochure and a **partner-with-us** page.
- **PayPal donations** and a sponsor wall of 25+ corporate supporters.

## Stack

WordPress with Elementor, and PayPal for donations.
