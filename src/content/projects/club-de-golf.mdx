## Overview

Club De Golf is a café and driving range in Newtown's Eco Park. The site serves three audiences at once: diners, golfers and people booking private events.

## What was built

- **Menu, Events and a "Golf for Everyone" page** explaining pay-per-bucket sessions.
- **Delivery ordering** through Zomato and Swiggy from a persistent call-to-action.
- An **"In the Spotlight" press section** featuring The Telegraph and LBB coverage.
- An **enquiry popup** for table bookings and private parties, plus a blog.

## Stack

WordPress with Elementor.
