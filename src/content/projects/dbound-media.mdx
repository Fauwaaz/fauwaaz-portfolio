## Overview

DBound Media is a communications agency covering digital and social media, PR, creative production and leadership profiling. The site has to do what the agency sells: tell a story visually before anyone reads a word.

## What was built

- A **video-first homepage**: an intro reel, then each service line presented with its own looping clips instead of static imagery.
- **Industry showcase** covering eleven sectors, from food and beverage to government and NGOs.
- A **client wall** of 30+ brand logos and dedicated About, Services, Projects and Contact pages.
- **Media served from a separate CDN subdomain**, keeping heavy video off the main application.

## Stack

A Next.js frontend with content and media managed in WordPress, plus Meta Pixel for campaign tracking.
