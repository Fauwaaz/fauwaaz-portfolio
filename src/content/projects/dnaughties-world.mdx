## Overview

dNaughties World runs preschool, daycare, after-school and event programmes for children aged 1.5 to 6. Parents choosing a preschool look for trust first, so the site is built around proof: facilities, educators and real reviews.

## What was built

- **Programme pages** for preschool, daycare, after-school and events, with age-banded classes from pre-nursery to senior KG.
- An **admissions page**, **gallery**, **testimonials** and a **book-a-visit** flow.
- **Verified Google reviews** embedded on the homepage and an Instagram feed.
- A **blog** for local search around "preschool in Mulund West".

## Stack

WordPress with Elementor, and Google Tag Manager for tracking.
