## Overview

House of R-Martin sells menswear across the UAE, priced in AED, with fast local delivery, cash on delivery and seven-day returns. The store has to feel like a fashion brand while handling a catalogue of jeans, shirts, linen, co-ords and accessories.

## What was built

- **Twelve collections** (bestsellers, linen, essentials, co-ord sets and more) filterable from one shop view.
- **Variant-aware product pages**, so each size and colour has its own addressable URL and ratings.
- A **cart**, **WhatsApp support**, an **affiliate programme** and a **blog** for search traffic.
- **Product imagery on Cloudflare R2**, optimised on the fly through Next.js image handling.
- **Error monitoring with Sentry** in production.

## Stack

Next.js with images stored on Cloudflare R2, Sentry for monitoring and Google Tag Manager for analytics.
