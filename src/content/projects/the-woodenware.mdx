## Overview

The Woodenware sells handcrafted wooden kitchenware, home decor, organisers and corporate gifts. The store has to carry a premium, sustainable feel while staying a straightforward shop.

## What was built

- A **WooCommerce shop** with category filters for home decor, kitchenware and storage.
- **Product pages** with sale pricing, plus a **wishlist**, **order tracking** and customer accounts.
- A **Book Online** section for services alongside physical products.
- Shoppable "Shop the Look" blocks, a video banner and a newsletter signup with a first-order discount.

## Stack

WordPress, WooCommerce and Elementor, with Meta Pixel for ad tracking.
